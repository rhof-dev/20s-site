20S

20 Strong Companion App
UNOFFICIAL

Support

20S is a free, fan-made companion for the solo game 20 Strong. It tracks the Stat Dice, the Counter Chip and the Hit Dice, and keeps a log of your plays.

Questions, bugs or ideas: email rhof.dev@gmail.com. Please include your phone model and iOS version.

Can I see the tour again?

Yes. On the Tracker, tap the gear (Settings) › Show the Tracker tour.

Why doesn't the app check the rules?

On purpose. The app only stands in for the components, so you play exactly as you would at the table.

How do I get my plays onto twentystrongstats.com?

Open a play in the Game Log and tap Submit to 20S Stats. The app shows the values in the site's own wording and opens the site's form in Safari for you to fill in. twentystrongstats.com is a separate community site.

Will it stop me submitting a play twice?

Once you've opened the form for a play, it's marked ✓ 20S Stats in your Game Log, and the app warns you before you submit it again. If you didn't actually send it, tap "Mark as not submitted".

Will I lose my plays if I change phones?

Your data is part of your phone's normal iCloud or Android backup. Restore that backup on the new phone to bring your plays across.

Privacy policy

20S collects no data.

  • Everything you enter (your Tracker state, plays, notes and settings) is stored only on your device, and in that device's own backups if you use them.
  • The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data. It makes no network requests.
  • Submit to 20S Stats only opens twentystrongstats.com in your browser. What you enter there is covered by that site's own policies.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of its data.

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app is. Last updated 14 October 2026.
